Sunway final year projects

I supervise final year projects in optimization and Operations Research only. I don’t take students at random. I’m looking for students who come with their own starting idea.

You don’t need a full proposal or method. That’s where I come in, and I’ll help you shape it. I just want the first idea to come from you. Here are three ways to find one:

Improve Sunway

A real-world problem

Something in Sunway City or Sunway University that could run better, like timetables, queues, transport, energy, or resource planning.

Follow your passion

Something you love

Tell me what you're passionate about, from sports to gaming to music, and I'll help you find the optimization problem inside it.

Go academic

A research paper

Find a recent paper in a top Operations Research or optimization journal and tell me you want to build on it.

How it works

  1. Find your idea

    Even a rough one is fine.

  2. Tell me about it

    Email me a few lines, or book a time.

  3. Meet and decide

    We meet first, and then we both decide if we're a good fit.

Ph.D. and M.Sc.

I’m looking for Ph.D. and M.Sc. students in the fields below. Full scholarships and funding are available for top students with strong qualifications.

Field

Operations Research and Optimization

Field

Scheduling, Timetabling, and Metaheuristics

Field

Game Theory and Multi-Agent Systems

What I look for

  • A strong educational background from a top university in your country
  • Experience in Operations Research, shown by a thesis or publications
  • Good programming skills, preferably in Java and Python
  • A solid foundation in the mathematics of optimization
  • Upper intermediate or advanced English, with at least IELTS 6.0 or equivalent

How to apply

Email me about your educational background, your supervisors, your experience, and your plans for postgraduate study.

Use this subject line: “Prospective [Ph.D. or M.Sc.] Student - Your Name”. It shows me you’ve read these instructions, so I don’t review emails with a different subject.

Please attach:

Required now
  • Your CV
  • Your official transcript (the latest one if you're in your final year)
  • Your degree certificates (skip if you're in your final year)
  • Proof of English proficiency (skip if you're a native speaker or studied in English)
Later
  • A research proposal
  • 2 recommendation letters

Not needed at first, but required for the final application.

Optional
  • Abstracts of your thesis or publications

Not required, but helpful.

I read every application, but because there are so many, I may only reply to shortlisted candidates to set up a meeting. If you’re accepted, you’ll then apply officially through the university portal before the deadline.

I took part in the European Space Agency's Space Optimisation Competition (SpOC 4) at GECCO 2026, a set of space logistics optimization challenges. Our team ranked 4th globally in one track, using permutation encodings and simulated annealing.

Our solutions were published as two GECCO 2026 papers.

[Visit the project](https://www.esa.int/gsp/ACT/news/spoc-2026/)

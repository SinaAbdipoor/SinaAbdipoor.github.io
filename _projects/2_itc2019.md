An implementation of the International Timetabling Competition 2019 benchmark, the latest benchmark for university course timetabling with student sectioning. It is the core of my Ph.D. and the base of our papers on optimality, generality, and distance to feasibility.

## Papers

- [Optimality Versus Generality: Performance Assessment of Meta-Heuristics in Educational Timetabling](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3565522), IEEE Access, 2025
- [Distance to Feasibility: Performance Assessment of Baseline Metaheuristics on University Course Timetabling with Student Sectioning](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICSPC68261.2025.11325835), ICSPC 2025
- [Meta-heuristic approaches for the University Course Timetabling Problem](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.iswa.2023.200253), Intelligent Systems with Applications, 2023
- [International Timetabling Competition 2019: A Systematic Literature Review](https://doi.org/10.1109/I2CACIS57635.2023.10193383), I2CACIS 2023

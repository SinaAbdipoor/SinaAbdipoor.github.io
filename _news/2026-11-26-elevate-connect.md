As a member of the Student Engagement & Experience Committee and an Academic Success Coach, I co-organized Elevate & Connect, a welcome event for new first-year students of the Faculty of Engineering and Technology. We helped them get to know Sunway University, meet their FET community, and learn about our Academic Success Coach program. The event was supported by the Jeffrey Cheah Foundation.

For me, the first weeks of university are all about relationships. When students feel they belong and stay in the loop, everything else gets easier. It was great spending time with all the new students and getting to know them, and even better to see them connect with their own academic success coaches.

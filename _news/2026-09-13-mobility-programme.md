I served on the Student Engagement & Experience Committee of the Sunway University Mobility Programme in AI and Automation. We hosted 9 students from Galgotias University (India) and Óbuda University (Hungary), together with 6 Sunway University students, for a week of CDIO, AI, and Automation workshops. By the end of the week, the students worked in groups to propose solutions to sustainability issues in their home countries.

The Faculty of Engineering and Technology gave me a Certificate of Appreciation for my part in organizing it.

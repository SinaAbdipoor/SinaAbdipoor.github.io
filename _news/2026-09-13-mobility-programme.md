As a member of the Student Engagement & Experience Committee, I helped organize the Sunway University Mobility Programme in AI and Automation. For one week, we hosted 9 students from Galgotias University (India) and Óbuda University (Hungary), who joined 6 Sunway University students in workshops on CDIO, AI, and automation. To wrap up the week, the students worked in teams to propose solutions to sustainability challenges in their home countries.

The Faculty of Engineering and Technology awarded me a Certificate of Appreciation for my role in the program.

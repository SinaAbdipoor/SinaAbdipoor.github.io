## Book a meeting

You can [book a time with me here](https://outlook.office.com/bookwithme/user/776cdb1789e842c8aa4e6009fdf1fe90@sunway.edu.my/meetingtype/V6T55AaInUyInzSwXI6JsA2?anonymous&ismsaljsauthenabled&ep=mlink). In the "Notes" box, please include:

- whether the meeting is "Online" or "In Person"
- the meeting topic and purpose
- "Group" if more than one person is joining
- the agenda, so we can both come prepared

A few things to keep in mind:

- Timeslots are limited, so please only book if it can't be discussed in class or over a Teams message.
- Check your calendar 2 hours before the meeting. I will only attend if my RSVP is yes.
- If you need to cancel, do it at least 8 hours before. Cancel it in your Teams calendar and send me a Teams message.
- For an in person meeting, check with me on Teams first that I'm in my office that day.

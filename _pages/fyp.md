## What I supervise

I work in optimization and Operations Research, so I only supervise projects in this area. I don't accept students at random. I'm looking for students who bring their own starting idea.

## Bring an idea

You don't need a full proposal or a methodology. That's where I come in, and I'll help you shape it. I just want the first idea to come from you. Here are three ways to find one:

<div class="idea-grid">
  <div class="tile">
    <span class="lbl">Improve Sunway</span>
    <h3>A real-world problem</h3>
    <p>Something in Sunway City or Sunway University that could run better, like timetables, queues, transport, energy, or resource planning.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tile">
    <span class="lbl">Follow your passion</span>
    <h3>Something you love</h3>
    <p>Tell me what you're passionate about, from sports to gaming to music, and I'll help you find the optimization problem inside it.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tile">
    <span class="lbl">Go academic</span>
    <h3>A research paper</h3>
    <p>Find a recent paper in a top Operations Research or optimization journal and tell me you want to build on it.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## How it works

1. Find your idea, even if it's rough.
2. [Email me](mailto:sinaa@sunway.edu.my) a few lines about it, or [book a time](/contact/).
3. We meet first. After that, we both decide if we're a good fit.

I'm a Lecturer in the Department of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at Sunway University, Malaysia. I use Operations Research, Optimization, and Machine Learning to solve real-world problems, especially scheduling and timetabling. I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Universiti Putra Malaysia.

My research focuses on metaheuristics and hybrid algorithms for combinatorial and stochastic optimization. I have published in IEEE Access, Intelligent Systems with Applications, and GECCO, and I have won two Best Paper Awards. I collaborate with researchers in Malaysia and China.

I have taught since 2016, first English and later computer science and AI. Today I teach machine learning and software engineering, and I supervise Master's and undergraduate research.

## Available positions

I am recruiting postgraduate (Ph.D. and M.Sc.) students in the following fields. Full scholarships and funding are available for top students with high qualifications.

- Artificial Intelligence, Operations Research
- Optimization, Scheduling, Timetabling
- Evolutionary Computing, Metaheuristics

## Requirements

- A strong educational background from top universities in your country
- Familiarity with operations research, demonstrated by a thesis or publications
- Proficiency in programming, preferably in Java and Python
- A solid foundation in mathematics related to optimization
- An upper-intermediate or advanced level of English, with a minimum score of IELTS 6.0 or equivalent

## How to apply

Send me an [email](mailto:sinaa@sunway.edu.my) with a description of your educational background, the names of your supervisors, your previous experience, and your plans for your postgraduate studies.

The subject of the email must follow this template: "Prospective [Ph.D. or M.Sc.] Student - Your Name". This shows you have read these instructions. If the subject line is incorrect, your application will not be reviewed.

Attach the following documents:

- CV
- Official transcript (send the latest transcript if you are a final-year student)
- Academic degrees (optional if you are a final-year student)
- Research proposal (not required in the first stage but necessary for the final application)
- 2 recommendation letters (not required in the first stage but necessary for the final application)
- Proof of English proficiency (optional if you are a native speaker or graduated from a university where English is the medium of instruction)
- Thesis or publication abstracts (optional but helpful)

I review all applications, but due to the high volume, I might only reply to shortlisted candidates to schedule a meeting. If you are accepted, you will need to complete the official application through the university portal before the deadline.
